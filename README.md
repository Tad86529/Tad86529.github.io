## Profile

I am a self-motivated, results-oriented industry professional and academic with a strong grasp of management and financial accounting procedures in both Australian public and private sector businesses. I believe accounting is more than just rules and numbers. Every financial statement tells a story about a business, its health, its values, and its future. My goal is to change how we teach accounting: instead of just focusing on technical steps, I help students learn how to find these hidden stories in the data. In my classroom, we go beyond debits and credits. I teach students how to listen to what the numbers are telling us and use that understanding to make smarter business decisions.
